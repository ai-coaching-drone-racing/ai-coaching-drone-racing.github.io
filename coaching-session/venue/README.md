# Warehouse Visual Material

`matte-floor.png` was generated with the built-in image generation tool on
2026-09-10 for the browser demo. It is a new visual texture, not an alteration
of the source warehouse textures and not part of the physics export. The
renderer blends it into the selected surface color at low contrast.

Generation prompt:

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless tileable PBR albedo texture
for the floor of a realistic indoor warehouse drone racing room. Generate one
square 1024x1024 texture, orthographic straight-down scan of clean matte sealed
fine concrete / smooth gray industrial resin floor, neutral medium-light gray,
very subtle natural fine aggregate grain and faint mottling only. This is a
texture swatch filling the entire image edge to edge, NOT a picture of a room.
Looks physically real and pleasantly clean, not distressed, not plastic, not
shiny. Extremely low contrast evenly distributed microtexture. No directional
lighting, no illumination gradient, no reflections, no shadows, no seams, no
panels, no cracks, no lines, no large patches, no dirt stains, no objects, no
text, no border, no watermark. Opposite edges should tile seamlessly. Uniform
diffuse daylight as in a cross-polarized material scan.

The original scene textures, meshes, gates and policies retain their original
ownership and provenance in `../sim/asset-provenance.json`.
